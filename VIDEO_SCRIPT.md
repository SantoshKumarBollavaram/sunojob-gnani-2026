# SunoJob: 2-3 minute submission demo

Target 2 minutes 40 seconds to 2 minutes 55 seconds, within the form's 2-3
minute requirement. This is an editing plan, not a measured application latency.
Record a complete working flow first. Cut provider waiting time if necessary,
label it "Waiting time shortened", and preserve the actual outputs and approvals.
Do not splice a different request's receipt into the demonstrated request.

## 0:00-0:20 | Introduce the problem

Show the app. Say:

“Hi, I'm Santosh Kumar Bollavaram from PES University. I built SunoJob to help
customers and service businesses review spoken work instructions before work
begins. The important details are often the quantity, what must not be done,
and what needs permission.”

## 0:20-0:45 | Speak and inspect

Choose English, start a fresh request and record this into the application:

“Prepare fifty, actually forty lunch boxes. Do not add onion. Ask me before
changing the menu.”

Once the real card appears, say:

“Gnani Prisma transcribes my speech, and Gemini extracts the structured draft.
Here are forty lunch boxes, no onion, and permission required before changing
the menu. The card also links back to the original words.”

Do not say the draft is correct until you actually check those values.

## 0:45-1:10 | Play readback

Click Generate spoken readback. Say this before playback:

“Gnani Timbre reads the current card back to me. This lets me listen to the
interpretation before I confirm it.”

Let the readback play with no narration over it. Complete playback is required
in the app. Keep the relevant readback audible in the published video.

## 1:10-1:40 | Correct and review again

Record this into the application:

“Make that forty-five lunch boxes. Keep the other instructions.”

Once the real revised card appears, say:

“The quantity is now forty-five. Both restrictions must still be present.
This is a new version and needs a new readback and confirmation.”

Generate and listen fully to the revised readback, then record:

“I confirm.”

If the provider uses the recovery request, wait for its real result. If the
card stays pending or the restriction disappears, stop and fix that failure
before calling this a completed demonstration.

## 1:40-2:05 | Separate owner acceptance

Create and open the owner-review link on the laptop. Say:

“The service provider reviews the same version separately. I'm acting as
both roles for this demonstration. This local prototype does not verify
the person's identity.”

Enter a fictional demo name, review every instruction, tick the review checkbox
and accept. Do not expose the full private review link in the published video.

## 2:05-2:30 | Show the shared record

Download the receipt for that exact request and version. Say:

“Both approval records refer to this version. The shared record includes the
instructions, correction history and readable timestamps. It can be downloaded
or emailed to both intended recipients after a preview and explicit send.”

Email delivery is optional in this short recording. Only say you sent or received
these copies if you demonstrate or check that exact attempt. Hide real inbox
details and addresses. A submitted SMTP result alone is not inbox confirmation.

## 2:30-2:50 | Technology and honest close

Show the README or one-page pitch. Say:

“The stack uses Gnani Prisma and Timbre, Gemini, FastAPI and SQLite. The app
supports English and Telugu examples. The patched source passes two hundred
and thirty-six offline tests. These are software checks, not speech-accuracy
benchmarks. Next, I will test unfamiliar speakers and real service workflows.”

If a Telugu clip is included, label it as a separate example and keep the
whole final video under 3 minutes. The form does not require two languages
in a single video; a complete honest workflow takes priority.
