## LinkedIn draft

Spoken instructions can change halfway through a sentence. A transcript alone
doesn't tell both parties which version they agreed to.

I built SunoJob for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge: an English/Telugu
voice workflow that turns a request into a reviewable job card, reads it back,
accepts corrections and saves a shared record after separate customer and
owner approval.

The demo shows a catering request: “fifty, actually forty lunch boxes,” no
onion, and permission required before changing the menu. A later correction
to 45 must preserve those restrictions and go through review again.

Gnani AI Prisma handles speech recognition, Timbre generates the spoken
readback, and Gemini extracts the structured draft. The app uses Python,
FastAPI and SQLite, with optional email copies of the approved record.

The local prototype passes 236 offline tests. I've also checked English/Telugu
examples and delivery to controlled inboxes. Broader speech accuracy and
real-world usefulness still need independent testing.

The attached video shows the working application. I'd welcome feedback on
whether the review and correction steps make the agreed instructions clearer.
